# UNBIASED TRADES ENGINE RULEBOOK

## CORE PHILOSOPHY
- Trade WITH institutions, not against them
- Price reacts to liquidity and large prints
- Levels are more important than indicators
- Confirmation over prediction

---

## TIMEFRAME MODEL
- 4H = structure (city view)
- 15m = setup formation (street view)
- 5m = entry confirmation (front door)
- 1m = optional precision only

---

## KEY LEVELS
- Previous Day High / Low
- Premarket High / Low
- Major support/resistance zones
- Institutional print levels

---

## SETUPS

### CALLS
1. Break and hold above key level
2. VWAP reclaim + hold
3. Retest and continuation

### PUTS
1. Rejection at resistance
2. Failed bounce (lower high)
3. VWAP rejection

---

## CONFIRMATION RULES
- Must confirm at key level
- Volume supports move
- No chasing extended moves
- Prefer retests over first touch

---

## RISK RULES
- Risk per trade must be defined before entry
- No trade without invalidation level
- Avoid trading during low volume chop
- Reduce size in uncertain conditions

---

## ALERT LOGIC

### FREE
- Levels only
- Basic direction bias

### PREMIUM
- Exact entry zones
- Confirmation explanation
- Institutional reasoning

---

## EXECUTION RULES
- No trade without confirmation
- No overtrading midday chop
- Focus on open (9:30–10:30) and power hour (3–4)

---

## MACRO OVERLAY
- Oil ↑ = bearish pressure on SPY/QQQ
- Oil stabilization = potential reversal
- Geopolitical events override technicals

---

## SYSTEM RULES FOR CODEX
- Do NOT change strategy logic
- Do NOT invent new setups
- Only implement and optimize existing logic
- Always preserve risk controls
- Do NOT create new strategies without explicit instruction
- Always follow confirmation + risk rules before execution logic
- If logic is unclear, ask instead of assuming
- Do NOT execute trades without passing confirmation + risk rules
- Do NOT bypass pre-trade validator or portfolio risk checks
- Maintain separation between analysis, decision, and execution layers
- All alerts must reflect actual system reasoning (no fabricated logic)
- If any instruction conflicts with this rulebook, follow the rulebook
